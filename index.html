Our Philosophy

The Science of Mindful Mastication

ChewsToLose was founded on a simple biological truth: your body possesses an innate satiety signaling mechanism that modern fast-paced eating has overridden.

Hormones: Leptin & Ghrelin

When food enters the stomach, two major chemical messengers dictate your hunger levels: Ghrelin (the hunger hormone) and Leptin (the satiety signal).

When you swallow hastily without thorough chewing, food enters the stomach before the digestive track can stimulate CCK (Cholecystokinin) and PYY hormones. By extending your meal to at least 20 minutes with 25+ chews per bite, you allow these signals to reach the brain's hypothalamus, shutting off hunger cravings naturally.

Ghrelin Reduction
Slower eating causes a faster drop in hunger hormones, preventing secondary snacking.
Salivary Amylase
Chewing mixes food with enzymes, breaking down starches and eliminating digestive gas.
